# Interview 05: Colby

Freshman, Business Management. PMA member since September. Interviewed Nov 5, 2026, 15 minutes, over video call.

**PMA PM:** Thanks for jumping on. How do you use the PMA website?

**Colby:** Honestly? Mostly the game. Cougar Flight. I'm on the leaderboard. A bunch of us in my hall compete on it.

**PMA PM:** Nice. What about the rest of the site?

**Colby:** I check events sometimes. And I looked at the hackathon page a lot after that email went out, everyone did. My whole group chat was on it.

**PMA PM:** Have you used Resources?

**Colby:** Yeah, a few times. It's fine. I think it needs a lot more stuff though. Like, a lot more. There's only a few links in each category. Other clubs have way bigger lists. If I were you guys I'd add like a hundred more resources. More is better, right?

**PMA PM:** Which resources have you used?

**Colby:** Um. I think I opened the Cursor one? I don't really remember. I haven't needed most of it yet, I'm a freshman. But I know people want more.

**PMA PM:** What makes you say people want more?

**Colby:** Just a feeling. Everyone says the site could be better.

**PMA PM:** Is there anything that bothers you about the site?

**Colby:** Okay, yes. Dark mode. It should be the default. I'm on my phone at like midnight and the page is just white. I've told you guys like five times in the feedback box. Also the mobile menu. It's annoying. It takes me a couple taps to get anywhere.

**PMA PM:** Do you know where the dark mode toggle is?

**Colby:** I think there is one, yeah. But it should just be on. Nobody uses light mode.

**PMA PM:** Got it. Why do you think people would use the site less lately?

**Colby:** Oh, for sure it's the design. Like, the site looks kind of dated in light mode. And honestly the hackathon took over everything. After that email everyone was on the hackathon page and not the rest of the site. I bet that's what happened to your numbers.

**PMA PM:** Have you been through PM recruiting yet?

**Colby:** Not yet. I'm thinking about it for next year. I'll probably use Resources more then.

**PMA PM:** When you have used Resources, did you search for anything?

**Colby:** Maybe once? I don't remember what. I mostly just scroll.

**PMA PM:** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Colby:** Dark mode by default. And fix the mobile menu. And add a lot more resources. Okay, that's three things. But dark mode for sure.

**PMA PM:** That's helpful. Anything else?

**Colby:** Add more prizes to Cougar Flight. That's not really a site thing, but you asked.

**PMA PM:** Ha, noted. Thanks, Colby.
