# Interview 01: Maren

Senior, Information Systems. PMA member for two years. Interviewed Nov 3, 2026, 20 minutes, over video call.

**PMA PM:** Thanks for doing this. Can you tell me a little about where you are in recruiting?

**Maren:** Yeah, so I'm a senior. I did a PM internship this past summer and I'm waiting to hear back on the return offer, but I'm also doing a few APM loops just in case. So I'm deep in it right now.

**PMA PM:** How often do you use the PMA site?

**Maren:** Honestly, a couple times a week, sometimes more. I have the Resources page bookmarked. During September I was on it almost every day.

**PMA PM:** Walk me through the last time you used it.

**Maren:** Last night, actually. I opened Resources, clicked into APM Job Postings, and opened APM List because I check which programs flipped to open. That's like a ritual. Then I went back and opened Aced because I had a mock scheduled with someone from Product Haven and I wanted to look at the Google company guide first.

**PMA PM:** Do you use the search box at all?

**Maren:** Not really. I kind of know where everything lives at this point. It's like four categories, so I just click the one I want. I think I used search once to find the Cursor link and it worked fine.

**PMA PM:** What about the Practice Interviews section?

**Maren:** Yeah, I've used that a lot. I like the guide part a lot, the "what it tests" and "what hurts" lists. That's basically what my mentor told me last year. For videos, I usually just go to the topic I'm prepping and watch the ones marked Start here. I'll watch a whole 30 minute mock while I make dinner. I've probably watched most of the Product Sense ones by now.

**PMA PM:** Is there anything that frustrates you on the site?

**Maren:** Not a lot, honestly. Maybe that I can't tell which videos I've already watched, so sometimes I click one and realize, oh, I've seen this. But it's minor. I'm kind of the person they built it for, I think.

**PMA PM:** What do you mean by that?

**Maren:** Like, I already know how recruiting works. I know what a product sense round is, I know when APM apps open. So the site is a shortcut for me. When I was a sophomore I don't think I would have known what any of this was for. I'd probably just have Googled stuff.

**PMA PM:** Have you told other people about it?

**Maren:** Yeah, I send the APM List link to people all the time. A couple of my friends in the club use it the same way I do. A few of the newer members have asked me where to start, and I usually just tell them to open Aced and the APM playbook part.

**PMA PM:** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Maren:** Probably nothing big. Maybe mark the videos I've watched. And maybe a reminder email when a big APM program opens, but APM List already does that, so I don't know if PMA needs to.

**PMA PM:** Anything else we should know?

**Maren:** Just that it's genuinely helpful. I know the officers put a lot into it. I'd keep it simple. Don't add a hundred more links. The good stuff is already there.

**PMA PM:** Thank you, that's really helpful.
