# Interview 02: Owen

Junior, Strategy. PMA member since September. Interviewed Nov 4, 2026, 15 minutes, in person after a club meeting.

**PMA PM:** Thanks for staying after. How's recruiting going?

**Owen:** It's starting to get real. I have a first round for a PM internship in like two weeks, and I've never done a product interview before. So I'm kind of panicking a little.

**PMA PM:** Have you used the PMA site to prep?

**Owen:** I tried. That's kind of why I said yes to this, actually.

**PMA PM:** Tell me about that.

**Owen:** So the recruiter told me the first round is a case interview, product sense type stuff. I went to Resources, and the first thing I saw was the search bar at the top. So I typed "case interview." It said no resources found. Then I typed "product sense," same thing. Then "behavioral" because I figured at least they'd have something for that. Nothing.

**PMA PM:** What did you do next?

**Owen:** I closed it and went to YouTube. I figured PMA just didn't have interview stuff yet, like maybe it was mostly job boards. I didn't really think about it after that.

**PMA PM:** Did you scroll down the page before you searched?

**Owen:** Not really. I mean, I went straight to the search box because that's what I do on every site. And once you type something, the page kind of changes to just the results, so I only saw the "no resources found" message.

**PMA PM:** Did you know there's a Practice Interviews section on that page?

**Owen:** Not until last week. My roommate showed me. He was like, dude, there's a whole tab for product sense with mock interviews. I was honestly kind of annoyed, because I'd been watching random YouTube videos for a week and some of them were not good.

**PMA PM:** How did that change how you use the site?

**Owen:** I mean, I know it's there now. But I'll be honest, I haven't really gone back since. I already have a YouTube playlist going. And I kind of assume if I search for something and it's not there, it's not there.

**PMA PM:** What would you have expected to happen when you searched "case interview"?

**Owen:** Just show me the stuff. Like the videos, or the Aced link, or a book, whatever you have. Even if it's not an exact match. I don't know what the officers call things. I don't know if it's called "product sense" or "product design" or "case." I'm a junior, I'm new to this.

**PMA PM:** Anything else you looked for that you couldn't find?

**Owen:** I searched "tell me about yourself" at one point because I knew that's always the first question. Nothing for that either. Later someone told me the book in there has a chapter on it.

**PMA PM:** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Owen:** Make search actually find things. That's it. I think a lot of people in my year are doing exactly what I did. We search, we get nothing, we leave.

**PMA PM:** That's really useful. Thank you.

**Owen:** Yeah, no problem. If you fix it, let me know, I still have that first round.
