# Interview 03: Anjali

Sophomore, Computer Science. Signed up for a PMA account in October. Interviewed Nov 4, 2026, 15 minutes, over video call.

**PMA PM:** Thanks for taking the time. How did you first hear about PMA?

**Anjali:** At the opening social in October. Someone at the table said if you join you get access to all the interview prep and the APM deadline list. I'm thinking about switching from software engineering recruiting to PM, so that sounded perfect.

**PMA PM:** What did you do after that?

**Anjali:** I made an account on the website that night, on my phone. Then I clicked Resources and it said members only. It said something like my account isn't a verified member yet, pay dues to unlock it.

**PMA PM:** Did you pay dues?

**Anjali:** Yeah, right then. There's a button that takes you to the BYU clubs page. I paid, went back to the site, and it was still locked. I figured it just needed a minute to update.

**PMA PM:** And then?

**Anjali:** I checked the next day, still locked. Checked again maybe two days later, still locked. I sent an email to the club address asking if I did something wrong. I don't think I ever heard back, or maybe I did and it went to spam, I'm not sure.

**PMA PM:** How did that feel?

**Anjali:** Honestly it was kind of a bummer. I was really excited at the social and then it just felt like the site didn't work. It wasn't a big deal, I just moved on. I had a bunch of midterms that week.

**PMA PM:** Did you ever get access?

**Anjali:** I think so? Someone told me later that an officer approves everyone by hand, so it probably went through at some point. But I haven't been back to check. By then I'd already found some free stuff on other sites and I sort of forgot about it.

**PMA PM:** Did you know that approval is done by hand?

**Anjali:** No. Nothing on the page said that. It said pay dues, so I paid dues. If it had said "an officer will approve you within a week" I would have at least known to wait. Or if it emailed me when it was unlocked, I would have come right back.

**PMA PM:** What were you hoping to use it for?

**Anjali:** The APM deadlines mostly. I heard some programs close really fast. And interview prep, because I've never done a PM interview. I still haven't really started on that.

**PMA PM:** Do you know other people who had the same experience?

**Anjali:** My friend who signed up the same night had the exact same thing. She was asking me if it worked for me. I think she gave up faster than I did.

**PMA PM:** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Anjali:** Just let me in when I sign up. Or let me in once I pay. I get that you want people to pay dues, but the waiting part is where you lose people. I was the most motivated I've been about PM on that first night.

**PMA PM:** That's really helpful. Is there anything else?

**Anjali:** No, I think that's it. I'll go check if it works now, actually.
