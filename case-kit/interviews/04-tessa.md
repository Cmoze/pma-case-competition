# Interview 04: Tessa

Junior, Marketing. PMA member since last winter. Interviewed Nov 5, 2026, 20 minutes, in person.

**PMA PM:** Thanks for meeting. Where are you in recruiting right now?

**Tessa:** I applied to a bunch of PM internships in September, and I just got my first interview. It's a Google style product round, next Thursday. So I have about a week.

**PMA PM:** Have you used the PMA site to prepare?

**Tessa:** Yes, I went to the Practice Interviews section on Resources. I knew it was there because an officer mentioned it at a meeting.

**PMA PM:** Walk me through what you did.

**Tessa:** So it opens on Product Sense, which is what I needed, so that part was great. I read the guide at the top, the what it tests and how to approach it part. That was really clear, actually. Then I scrolled down to the videos.

**PMA PM:** What happened there?

**Tessa:** There are like sixteen of them. And they're all mock interviews, and they're all 25, 30, 40 minutes. Two of them say Start here, but those are also full mocks, like 25 and 29 minutes. I opened one, it's two people on a call starting a whole interview, and I was like, I don't have 30 minutes right now, I just want to know what a good answer looks like. So I closed it.

**PMA PM:** What did you do after closing it?

**Tessa:** I opened a different one, thinking maybe it was shorter or more like an overview. Same thing. I think I opened four or five and closed all of them within a few seconds. They all look the same. "PM Mock: Improve this, Meta PM Mock: design that."

**PMA PM:** What were you hoping to find?

**Tessa:** Something that matched where I am. I'm a week out from my first ever round. I don't need the 45 minute one about designing for moving cities. I need, like, here's what a first round looks like, here are the two videos to watch this week, here's the one to watch the night before. Right now it's just a wall of videos and I have to guess.

**PMA PM:** Did you look at the other tabs?

**Tessa:** I clicked Interview Basics because I thought that might be the beginner stuff. It had some shorter ones, the rubric one was helpful. But I didn't know to go there first. Product Sense is the default, so that's where I started, and that's where I got overwhelmed.

**PMA PM:** Did you end up watching any of the long ones?

**Tessa:** One, on the weekend, the Headspace one. It was good. But that was after I'd already spent an hour on YouTube looking for something shorter.

**PMA PM:** How do you feel about the site overall?

**Tessa:** I like it. I don't want it to sound like it's bad. The content is great. It's just that there's a lot of it and nothing tells me which part is for me. My friend who's a senior just watches all of them, but she already knows what she's doing.

**PMA PM:** If you could change one thing, what would it be?

**Tessa:** Ask me where I am. Like, just starting, have an interview soon, or final rounds. Then show me three videos, not sixteen. And tell me how long each one takes so I can plan.

**PMA PM:** The tiles do show the minutes, right?

**Tessa:** Oh, they do, in the corner. I guess I mean tell me which ones are worth my time this week. Knowing it's 31 minutes doesn't help me pick.

**PMA PM:** Thank you, that's super helpful.
